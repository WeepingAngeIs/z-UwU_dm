# Model review

Check every new or revised model in the UwU Dimension for z-fighting. Inspect visible faces for coplanar overlap, including nested parts, trim and layered textures. Give visible layers real separation or remove the covered face.

On the hosted Minecraft client, inspect the packaged model from the front, side and rear, up close and at normal playing distance. Move the camera slightly at each distance to reveal flicker. For animated models, check idle, movement and any changed action, including transitions. Review the actual frames for unintended intersections. Keep representative views with the test evidence and record any unverified pose before calling the model finished.
